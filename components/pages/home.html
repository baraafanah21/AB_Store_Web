Born in Qalqilia,
Crafted for the World

AB Store began in the heart of Qalqilia — a city known for its orange groves, its patient craftsmanship, and a people who understand that the finest things are never rushed. We carry that same spirit into every bottle we sell.

Working directly with the historic ateliers of Paris, the avant-garde studios of Milan, and the storied perfumeries of the East, we hand-select fragrances that deserve a place on your dresser — authenticated, beautifully packaged, and delivered with the warmth of a Palestinian welcome.
